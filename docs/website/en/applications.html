Enquiries and forms

Create an information request, event registration or job application form. Responses remain available in the administration interface for review and further processing.

Forms, conditional questions and response reports
  • Forms: create single-step or multi-step forms with required fields and validation.
  • Conditional fields: show additional questions or make them required based on earlier answers.
  • Response management: view, filter and export submitted data. Create response charts for selected fields.
  • Encryption: protect form values stored in the database with a configured encryption key.
  • Polls and questionnaires: collect feedback and display voting results or responses.

Form editor documentation · Questionnaires

Documents and content

Keep annual reports, price lists and downloadable forms organised. Publish news, articles and photos in listings that fit your website structure.

Document Manager, news, blogs and galleries
  • Document Manager: manage documents, versions and categories. Schedule a new version for a future date and display a selected group of documents on a page.
  • News: create listings of news items, products or contact locations from your website content.
  • Blog: publish articles and manage their authors.
  • Gallery: upload photos and let the system prepare thumbnails and images at the configured sizes.

Document Manager documentation · News

Search and content access

Help visitors find information in a large portal. Make content for clients, partners or employees available after sign-in.

Search, language versions and restricted sections
  • Search: extend full-text search with semantic or hybrid mode and an AI answer based on the content found. These options require indexing and an AI provider.
  • Language versions: linked content and a language switcher take visitors to the corresponding page.
  • Restricted area: make content available to selected groups of registered users.

Search documentation · Restricted pages

Marketing and analytics

Publish a campaign, reach subscribers and track how visitors use your website. Marketing applications work with content managed in WebJET CMS.

Email campaigns, banners, statistics and A/B tests
  • Bulk email: prepare personalised messages and manage recipients, unsubscribes and campaign progress.
  • Banners: manage advertising placements and track impressions and clicks.
  • Statistics: analyse traffic and page usage.
  • A/B testing: compare page variants with a configured split of visitors.
  • SEO: use reports to assess search visibility, including visits from search engine crawlers.
  • Cookie consent and Scripts: let visitors choose cookie categories and load the corresponding scripts based on their choices.

Bulk email documentation · A/B testing

Events and bookings

Share information about training sessions and events, or let visitors book a time slot or venue. Manage records in the administration interface and display them on the relevant pages.

Events calendar and reservation system

The Events calendar lets you record and display events. The Reservation system manages bookable resources, time slots and incoming bookings, including approval. Booking reports are also available.

Events calendar documentation · Managing bookings

E-commerce

If your website also needs to sell, use applications for products, a shopping cart and orders. Store data remains available in the administration interface alongside your website content.

Products, orders, payments and sales reports

Manage products and orders and configure payment and delivery methods. Statistics show revenue, best-selling products and order breakdowns for a selected period. Payment and delivery options are configured to match the needs of each store.

Order documentation · E-commerce reports
