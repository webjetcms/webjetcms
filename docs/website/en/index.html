Prepare changes in advance. Decide what goes live.

Prepare a new offer, press release or updated terms ahead of publication day. In WebJET CMS, you can draft a page version, set its publication time and involve an approver according to your organisation's rules.

  • An editor prepares the draft; an approver compares the changes and decides whether to publish them.
  • Schedule a page to go live on a chosen date and time.
  • Find previous versions and their approval records in the page history.
  • Assign access to pages, files and applications according to each team member's role.

AI where you write and edit content

Ask an assistant to draft text, translate content or revise a selected paragraph. In PageBuilder, you can also start from source documents and refine the page through chat.

Your administrator selects providers and configures assistants for the team. For selected text tasks and search, you can also deploy local models on your own server.
